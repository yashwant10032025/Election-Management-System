About State Election Commission

The State Election Commission, U.T., Chandigarh, was constituted under the provisions of Article 243K and 243ZA of the Constitution of India. It is vested with the superintendence, direction, and control of the preparation of electoral rolls for, and the conduct of, all elections to the Municipal Corporation and Panchayati Raj Institutions in the Union Territory of Chandigarh.

Our mission is to conduct free, fair, and transparent elections to strengthen local self-governance. The Commission ensures that every eligible citizen is enrolled in the voter list and exercises their franchise without any fear or favor.

Online Services

Our Services

Comprehensive modules for managing the entire election process securely.

EVM Management

Tracks the logistics of Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) and VVPATs, including storage and dispatch.
Polling Party Management

Handles the deployment and management of polling teams (Presiding Officers, Polling Officers).
Polling Station Management

Manages voting locations, tracking building conditions, capacities, and sector officer assignments.
Electoral Rolls & Voter Slip Management

Manages, views, and updates the official voter lists for various wards and polling stations.
Online Complaints

Submit and track grievances or complaints related to the electoral process.
Online Permission

Apply for and manage online permissions for rallies, meetings, and vehicles.
Nominations

Tracks the filing, scrutiny, and withdrawal of candidate nomination papers.
Voter Turnout

Records and monitors live voter turnout percentages across wards on polling day.
Online Results

Post-polling screen used to compile counting data and declare official election results publicly.
Postal Ballot

Manages the issuance and tracking of postal ballots for service voters and election duty staff.
MCC Reports

Tracks violations and enforcement of the Model Code of Conduct (MCC).
Voter Slip Distribution

Entry and dashboard for monitoring the distribution of voter slips.
Randomization

Manages random allocation of election duties and resources to ensure fairness.
Statutory Forms

Digital repository for managing mandatory legal forms and documents required during the election.
Observer Reports

Portal for Election Observers to submit their independent reports and findings to the Commission.
85+ & PWD

Manages special facilities, transport, or home-voting options for senior citizens and Persons with Disabilities.
Min. Facilities (AMF)

Ensures all polling stations meet Assured Minimum Facilities standards like drinking water, ramps, and electricity.
Affidavit Analysis

Reviews and publishes the financial, criminal, and educational backgrounds declared by candidates.
